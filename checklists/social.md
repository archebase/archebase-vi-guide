# Social / press checklist

- [ ] Mode selected in the brief: `guided` for normal branded social, `creative` for platform-native/campaign work, `strict` only when formal VI compliance is requested
- [ ] `templates/brief.md` filled with platform, placement and export dimensions
- [ ] Primary judgment legible in the first frame at real feed size
- [ ] Actual square, vertical and horizontal crops tested separately, not assumed
- [ ] Mark and critical text inside the platform safe area; the safe-area source is recorded
- [ ] If a Logo is used, its V2 variant is resolved and unmodified; if omitted for a creative/platform-native asset, the decision is intentional and recorded
- [ ] Conclusion before metadata; no more than three hierarchy levels in `strict`; channel-native hierarchy is allowed in `guided`/`creative`
- [ ] Every quoted metric has a source; no unapproved customer or product claim
- [ ] Image, face, customer and third-party rights confirmed
- [ ] No confidential or customer data visible in any screenshot, chart or photo background
- [ ] Rendered at final platform dimensions and inspected after downscaling to thumbnail size
