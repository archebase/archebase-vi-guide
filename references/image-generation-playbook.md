# Image generation and sourcing playbook

Load with `references/visual-grammar.md`, `references/logo-asset-resolver.md` and `references/guide-page-evidence.md`.

This playbook covers images used inside ArcheBase material — whether generated by a model or sourced from a photographer, a stock library or an internal shoot.

## 1. Brief

Fill `templates/brief.md`. An image brief must add:

- what claim the image is carrying, and why an image is the right carrier,
- whether real physical-world evidence is available instead,
- the required aspect ratio and crop,
- the intended placement (full bleed, panel, background, thumbnail),
- the provenance requirement: generated, licensed, owned shoot, or supplied by a third party.

## 2. Prefer real evidence

Real physical-world data and imagery outrank generated imagery. A photograph of the actual robot, rig, site or sensor outranks a plausible-looking generated scene, and a real chart outranks a generated one.

Before generating, check whether an approved internal or shoot image already exists. Generated imagery is appropriate for texture, atmosphere and abstract background — not for depicting a specific customer site, a specific product capability, or a factual claim.

## 3. Prompt rules

- **Never generate the ArcheBase mark.** No prompt may request the logo, wordmark, graphic mark, or an approximation of it, and no generated output may contain one. Place the real asset from `assets/logos/` afterwards, through the resolver.
- Never generate on-image text. Models render letterforms unreliably, and generated text that resembles the brand type is a type-system violation. Add real text in the layout tool.
- Prompt for the composition the brief needs — one dominant field, room for the primary judgment, a calm area where type and the mark will sit.
- Avoid the generic "AI futuristic" register: random node graphs, circuit wallpaper, glow, rainbow palettes, 3D charts, decorative gradients, filler stripes and unrelated icon families.
- Do not prompt for a recognizable real person, a specific customer's facility, or a third-party mark.

## 4. Provenance and rights

Record for every image, in the decision record or the release report:

- source: model and version, stock library and asset ID, photographer and shoot, or supplier,
- license terms and any usage restriction,
- generation date and the prompt used, for generated images,
- the human reviewer who accepted the result.

An image with no recorded provenance may not be released. This is a blocker, not a `Minor`.

## 5. Review the generated output

Generated images are subject to the same gates as any other asset, plus:

- inspect for malformed lettering, text-like artifacts, deformed hands, impossible geometry or other visible generation defects,
- confirm no accidental logo, wordmark or third-party mark appears anywhere in the frame,
- confirm no real, identifiable person appears without a recorded release,
- confirm the image does not assert a factual claim the evidence does not support.

## 6. QA before release

- Inspect at final crop and final placement, not as a standalone file.
- Check that type and the mark remain legible over the image; if not, change the image or the variant rather than adding effects.
- Check the image at its smallest real usage size.
- Run `checklists/image.md`, then the four gates in `references/release-gates.md`.
