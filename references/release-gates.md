# Release gates

## Asset gate

Official current Logo, correct variant, clear space/minimum size checked or `待确认`; approved font/license/weight; image, face, customer and third-party rights.

## Design gate

One clear judgment; evidence dominates; approved tokens used semantically; actual-size readability; no overflow, low contrast, random decoration or generic AI wallpaper.

## Claims/Rights gate

Names, claims, metrics, units, time, sample and sources checked; no unapproved customer/product claims, confidential data or generated Logo/text.

## Export/QA gate

Source/content QA; technical validation; rendered page/frame inspection; responsive/crop/distance/accessibility checks; all findings assigned owner and impact.

Any unresolved brand naming, Logo, type, color-role, factual claim or rights issue blocks external release. Verdict must be `可发布`, `修复后复审`, or `阻塞，待确认`.
