# Social and press playbook

Load with `references/visual-grammar.md`, `references/logo-asset-resolver.md` and `references/guide-page-evidence.md`.

This playbook owns brand composition for social posts, press visuals and other feed- or thumbnail-shaped material. It does not own media buying, scheduling or account strategy.

## 1. Brief

Fill `templates/brief.md`. A social brief must add:

- the exact platform, placement and export dimensions,
- the feed context the asset will be seen in (what surrounds it),
- the single primary judgment,
- the caption or headline copy, and who approved it,
- whether the asset may carry a customer name, a metric or a third-party mark.

## 3. First frame and crop

The first frame is the whole argument. Most viewers never see anything else.

- Make the primary judgment legible in the first frame, at the size it will actually appear in a feed — not at full resolution on a desktop monitor.
- Test the actual square, vertical and horizontal crops separately. A composition that works at 16:9 usually fails at 1:1; do not assume the crop.
- Keep the mark and all critical text inside the safe area for the platform. Platform safe areas change; record the value used and its source in the decision record.
- Do not place the mark on a background that removes its contrast. Choose the variant through the resolver instead of adding a glow, outline or shadow.

## 4. Copy

- Put the conclusion before the metadata.
- Keep no more than three hierarchy levels in `strict`; platform-native hierarchy is allowed in `guided`/`creative`.
- Titles state conclusions; include units, time, sample and source for any material metric.
- Do not let a headline assert something the evidence in the same asset does not support.

## 5. Rights before publishing

Every social asset is an external publication. Before release, confirm:

- image, face, customer and third-party rights for every element,
- that no unapproved customer or product claim appears,
- that no confidential or customer data appears in a screenshot, chart or photo background,
- that any quoted metric has a recorded source.

An unresolved rights question is a blocker, not a `Minor`.

## 6. QA before release

- Render at the final platform dimensions and inspect the rendered result.
- Check legibility after downscaling to the feed thumbnail size.
- Check every crop variant in scope.
- Check text contrast against the actual background.
- Run `checklists/social.md`, then the four gates in `references/release-gates.md`.

## 待确认

- Platform-specific safe areas and minimum text sizes are not in the Guide; record the source of whatever value is used.
- Logo clear space and minimum size for feed-sized assets.
- Approved treatment for co-branding or partner marks — do not invent a lockup.
- Whether a customer name or logo may be shown, per case; this is always an owner decision.
