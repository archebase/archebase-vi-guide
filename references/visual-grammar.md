# Visual grammar

## Tokens

- `AB_BLUE_1` `#0032FF`: strongest brand recognition and primary structure
- `AB_BLUE_2` `#7172FA`: supporting hierarchy
- `AB_BLUE_3` `#619AFD`: secondary data layer
- `AB_BLUE_4` `#46CFFF`: meaningful highlight/data flow
- `AB_CHARCOAL` `#1E2124`: technical dark field and neutral text
- Chinese: 思源黑体; Latin: Poppins; the Guide displays Chinese 粗黑/黑/细黑 and Latin ExtraBold/SemiBold/Regular
- p.28 color ratio labels: `AB_BLUE_1` 50%, `AB_BLUE_2` 25%, `AB_BLUE_3` 10%, `AB_BLUE_4` 5% (90% labelled total); the remaining 10% is not assigned in the Guide — explicit evidence, not an automatic per-component allocation
- p.30–31 neutral background examples: `#1E2124` at 100% and 5%
- p.32 neutral text examples: `#1E2124` at 100%, 70% and 50%

## Rules

Use one dominant field, one structural blue, and at most one meaningful highlight. Let evidence dominate decoration. Use data flow, connected nodes, labeled modules, system boundaries and real physical-world imagery only when they explain the claim. Remove random nodes, circuit wallpaper, glow, rainbow palettes, 3D charts, decorative gradients, filler stripes and unrelated icon families.

Do not introduce orange as an official CTA or status color; the Guide text does not confirm that system. Keep the numeric ratio and neutral levels as confirmed evidence, but treat semantic role names, per-component allocation, exact compositing, clear-space/minimum size, font licensing/exact production files, and naming as `待确认`. See `assets/guide-evidence.json` for the evidence boundary.

## Composition

Use system statement, evidence-led, transformation flow, comparison/decision, or image-led narrative. Put conclusion before metadata. Keep no more than three hierarchy levels. Test real crop, size, contrast and viewing distance.
